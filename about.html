About Car Info

Our mission

Car Info is an informative website dedicated to helping people make confident, well-informed decisions about cars. Whether you're buying your first vehicle, maintaining your current one, or curious about electric vehicles and emerging automotive technology, we've got you covered.

We believe reliable automotive information should be accessible to everyone — no paywalls, no subscriptions, just clear and honest content you can trust.

Quick facts

  • Audience: Car buyers, owners, enthusiasts
  • Access: Completely free
  • Built with: HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript
